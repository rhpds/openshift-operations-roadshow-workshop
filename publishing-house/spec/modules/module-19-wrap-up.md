# Module 19 — Wrap Up

### Brief Overview

This brief closing module recaps the key themes covered across the workshop and provides curated resource links for continued learning. Participants review what they accomplished and receive pointers to documentation, community channels, and follow-up labs.

### Audience and Time

- **Target persona:** All workshop participants
- **Prerequisites:** Completion of one or more workshop modules
- **Estimated duration:** 5 minutes

### Learning Objectives

- Review key operational concepts covered across the workshop modules
- Identify resources for continued learning and deeper exploration

### Lab Structure

| Section | Title | Duration |
|---------|-------|----------|
| 1       | Workshop Recap | 2 min |
| 2       | Resources & Next Steps | 3 min |

### Detailed Steps

1. Review the summary of topics covered across the workshop modules
2. Browse the curated list of documentation links, community resources, and follow-up labs
3. Note any areas of interest for further self-paced study

### Key Takeaways

- The workshop covered cluster verification, application management, networking, identity, observability, virtualization, multi-cluster management, security, and developer tooling
- Red Hat documentation, interactive learning portals, and community channels provide paths for continued skill development
